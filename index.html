Welcome to the lesson!

In this lesson, you will learn and practise adjectives describing people's personalities and character traits. Complete the vocabulary exercises and then use the new words in conversation.

Vocabulary note: Conscientiousness is a noun meaning the quality of being careful, responsible and hardworking. Conscientious is the adjective.
Example: She is a conscientious student. Her conscientiousness helps her achieve good results.

03

Speaking Challenge

Обсуди вопросы, используй новую лексику и объясняй свою точку зрения.

Part A. Choose the right person

1. You are choosing a person to lead a team. Would you choose someone calm or talkative?
2. You have a serious problem and need advice. Would you turn to someone down-to-earth or imaginative?
3. You are planning a trip abroad. Would you rather travel with someone laid-back or conscientious? Why?
4. You are working on an important project. Would you rather have a reliable teammate or a resourceful teammate?
5. You are going through a difficult period. Would you prefer to spend time with someone kind or broad-minded?
6. You need to discuss a disagreement with a colleague. Would you prefer someone diplomatic or impetuous?
7. You are organising a big party. Would you rather invite an outgoing person or a reserved person to help you?
8. You are starting a business. Would you prefer to work with a creative person or a practical person?

Part B. Describe a person

Выбери одного человека из своего окружения (друг, родственник, коллега, преподаватель) и опиши его, используя минимум 8 слов из списка.

1. How would you describe this person's personality?
2. What are their strongest character traits?
3. Are they more outgoing or reserved?
4. Are they practical or imaginative?
5. Can you always rely on them?
6. How do they behave in stressful situations?
7. What do you admire most about them?
8. Is there anything about their personality you find difficult?

Part C. Agree or disagree?

Вырази своё мнение и объясни его. Постарайся использовать лексику урока.

1. Quiet people can be just as successful as outgoing people.
2. Impetuous people are more likely to succeed because they take risks.
3. A good leader should be calm, diplomatic and conscientious.
4. Being broad-minded is essential in today's world.
5. Being outgoing is more important than being intelligent.
6. Being hard-working does not necessarily make someone successful.
7. A reliable person is always better to work with than a creative person.
8. Being too laid-back can prevent people from achieving their goals.
Useful phrases for discussion:

I'd rather work with someone who is... I would prefer a person who... One of the most important qualities is... I get along best with people who... I wouldn't describe myself as... What I appreciate most about this person is... In my opinion... I see your point, but...
